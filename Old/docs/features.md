# Email Pilots Features

Email Pilots is a personalized email outreach software designed to act as a personal assistant, sending perfectly personalized emails one by one to avoid spam filters.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Gmail & Outlook Integration**
   - Connects instantly without complex SMTP setups.
   - Works safely directly with the user's primary inbox.

2. **Deep Personalization**
   - Uses merge tags like `{{Name}}` or `{{Company}}` to dynamically adapt every single message based on spreadsheet data.

3. **Attachments**
   - Supports sending unique PDFs, pitch decks, or invoices to each recipient on autopilot.

4. **Smart Scheduling & Send Limits**
   - Built-in rate limiting prevents users from being flagged as spam by spacing out the emails naturally.
   - Plans campaigns to go out at the perfect time in the recipient's timezone.

5. **Local-First Privacy**
   - The software runs locally on the user's machine.
   - Contacts, proprietary lead lists, and email content stay on the local device and are not stored in the cloud.

## Target Personas

- **Job Seekers:** Reach out to hiring managers with personalized intros without waiting for job boards.
- **Recruiters:** Contact candidates automatically and send highly personalized follow-ups without expensive LinkedIn Recruiter licenses.
- **Freelancers:** Pitch services to local businesses on autopilot to keep calendars booked.
- **Community Builders:** Manage clubs or neighborhood groups by sending personalized updates without relying on messy BCC or group threads.

## Workflow

1. **Connect:** Securely connect an existing Gmail or Outlook account.
2. **Upload:** Drop in an Excel or CSV spreadsheet containing contacts.
3. **Send:** Write the pitch using simple tags and hit send; the software handles natural pacing and sending.
